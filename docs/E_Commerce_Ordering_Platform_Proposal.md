# Project Overview

The proposed E-Commerce Ordering Platform is a modern web-based
application designed to help Samud Shabkat establish a professional
online presence by showcasing IT hardware products while enabling
customers to place orders through a simple and convenient online
ordering system.

The platform enables customers to browse products, view detailed
specifications, add products to their cart, register their personal and
company information, and complete checkout by selecting Takeaway / Pay
at Shop as the order method. No online payment gateway is required. Once
an order is placed, it will be recorded in the system and made available
to administrators through the order management dashboard. Customers will
also receive email notifications regarding their order and status
updates.

## Current Business Challenges

-   Limited online presence for showcasing and selling IT hardware
    products.
-   Customers must contact the business manually to place product
    orders.
-   Customer and company information is not collected in a centralized
    system.
-   Product catalog updates and order handling require manual
    communication.
-   No centralized digital platform for managing customer orders.
-   Difficulty tracking pending, confirmed, completed and cancelled
    orders.
-   Limited communication with customers regarding their order status.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed E-Commerce Ordering Platform provides a centralized digital
system where customers can browse IT hardware products, add products to
their cart, register their details, and complete the checkout process
using a Takeaway / Pay at Shop order method. Customers can place orders
online without making an online payment and complete the payment
directly at the shop when collecting their products.

Once an order is placed, the order information will automatically appear
in the administration dashboard. Administrators can review customer and
order details, manage order status, confirm payment after the customer
pays at the shop, and complete or cancel orders when required. Email
notifications will keep customers informed about important order
updates.

### Key Objectives

-   Build a modern online product catalog and ordering platform.
-   Enable customers to add multiple products to a shopping cart.
-   Provide a simple checkout process without an online payment gateway.
-   Allow customers to select Takeaway / Pay at Shop for their orders.
-   Collect and securely maintain customer and company information.
-   Centralize customer order management through the administration
    dashboard.
-   Enable administrators to manage order and payment status.
-   Send automated email notifications for order confirmation and status
    updates.
-   Simplify product, brand, category and customer management.
-   Build a scalable foundation for future e-commerce expansion.

## User Roles & Access Control

The platform will implement a secure Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
system to ensure that each user has access only to the features and
information relevant to their responsibilities.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Role                                Responsibilities
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Super Administrator                 Full system access including
                                      dashboard, administrator
                                      management, product management,
                                      categories, brands, customers,
                                      orders, payment status, system
                                      settings and platform
                                      configuration.

  Administrator                       Access to a dedicated
                                      administration dashboard with
                                      permissions to manage products,
                                      categories, brands, customers,
                                      orders and order status based on
                                      assigned privileges.

  Customer                            Browse products, manage shopping
                                      cart, register personal and company
                                      information, place orders, select
                                      Takeaway / Pay at Shop and receive
                                      order notifications.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The Super Administrator has the authority to create additional Super
Administrators and Administrators, assign appropriate roles and
permissions, and manage access across the platform. Super Administrators
and Administrators will have dedicated dashboards based on their
respective permissions, ensuring secure and organized management of the
platform.
